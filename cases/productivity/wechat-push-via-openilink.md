# Minis 结果推送到微信（openilink-hub）

**Push Minis Results to WeChat via openilink-hub**

> 💬 *From the Open Minis community — shared by **meng nimen** on 2026-03-25*

---

## 🎯 痛点 / Pain Point

**中文：** Minis 运行结果只能在 App 内查看，无法主动推送到常用通讯工具。

**English:** Minis results are only viewable inside the app — no way to proactively push to messaging tools.

---

## 💡 做了什么 / What It Does

**中文：** 使用 openilink-hub 开源中间件，将 Minis 在 iPad 上执行的任何结果（天气、新闻、健康报告等）推送到手机微信，实现跨设备信息分发。

**English:** Uses the openilink-hub open-source middleware to push any Minis output (weather, news, health reports) from iPad to WeChat on your phone — cross-device information delivery.
