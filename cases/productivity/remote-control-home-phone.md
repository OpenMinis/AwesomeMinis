# 远程控制家里手机执行任务

**Remote Control Home Phone to Run Tasks**

> 💬 *From the Open Minis community — shared by **朦胧 22:36** on 2026-03-24*

---

## 🎯 痛点 / Pain Point

**中文：** 出门在外时，家里的手机/iPad 上有 Minis 跑着，但无法远程触发任务。

**English:** When away from home, you can't trigger tasks on the Minis running on your home phone/iPad.

---

## 💡 做了什么 / What It Does

**中文：** 通过网络远程连接家里运行 Minis 的手机，在外出时也能触发任务执行，实现随时随地的自动化控制。

**English:** Remotely connects to a home phone running Minis over the network, triggering task execution from anywhere — enabling always-on automation even when away from home.

**中文：**
```
（在外时通过另一设备）SSH 连接到家里的手机，触发每日健康数据收集任务。
```

(From another device while away) SSH into my home phone and trigger the daily health data collection task.
