# One Sentence → Professional City Commercial Market Research Report

> 💬 *From [@wsvn53](https://x.com/wsvn53) · 2026-02-27 reply thread*

---

## 🎯 Pain Point

City commercial research usually requires buying professional reports from firms like JLL, Cushman & Wakefield, and Winshang, manually consolidating multiple datasets, and then creating charts in Excel or Python. It is expensive, slow, and difficult to do well.

---

## 💡 What It Does

Say one sentence to Minis. It searches authoritative sources such as JLL, Savills, and Winshang, writes analysis scripts, generates multi-dimensional visual charts, and outputs a complete market report with trend curves, district comparisons, category breakdowns, and vacancy-rate distributions.

Example: analyzing retail vacancy rates in Chengdu over the past few years:

- Key conclusion: vacancy rates peaked in Q4 2022
- Chunxi Road / Taikoo Li core commercial areas stayed around 4–7.5% long-term
- ⚠️ Divergence intensified: the gap between core and fringe commercial districts widened

---

| Skill | Purpose |

## 💬 Example Prompt

```text
Analyze the retail vacancy rate in Chengdu over the past few years.
Search JLL, Savills, Winshang, and other data sources online.
Generate trend charts and a commercial-district comparison report.
```

---

## ⚙️ Requirements

- [ ] No extra configuration required; Minis has built-in browser search and Python charting capabilities

---

## 💡 Tips

- Replace the city and metric, e.g. “Beijing office occupancy rate” or “Shanghai retail property rent trends.”
- Generated charts can be exported as PNG files for sharing or embedding in reports.
- Useful for real-estate investment analysis, commercial site selection, and industry research.
