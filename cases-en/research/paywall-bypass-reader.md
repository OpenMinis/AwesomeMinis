# Automated Paywall Bypass Article Reader

> 💬 *From the Open Minis community — shared by **oneasai** on 2026-03-22*

---

## 🎯 Pain Point

Quality long-form articles from outlets such as Caixin and The Wall Street Journal are often locked behind paywalls and cannot be read directly.

---

## 💡 What It Does

Give Minis a paywall-bypass script or ask it to look for accessible versions. Minis rewrites paywalled article URLs to public archive or mirror links when available, so the article can be read and summarized directly inside the conversation.

---

## 🛠 Skills Used

| Skill | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Built-in `browser_use` | Search for accessible mirrors or archive versions |

---

## 💬 Example Prompt

```text
This article is paywalled: [URL].
Please find a publicly accessible version and summarize the key points.
```

---

## ⚙️ Requirements

- [ ] No extra configuration required; Minis has built-in browser capabilities
- [ ] Availability depends on whether the content has been captured by a public archive or mirror
