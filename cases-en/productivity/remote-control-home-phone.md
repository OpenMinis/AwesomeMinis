# Remote Control Home Phone to Run Tasks

> 💬 *From the Open Minis community — shared by **Menglong** on 2026-03-24*

---

## 🎯 Pain Point

When away from home, you can't trigger tasks on the Minis running on your home phone/iPad.

---

## 💡 What It Does

Remotely connects to a home phone running Minis over the network, triggering task execution from anywhere — enabling always-on automation even when away from home.

---

## 🛠 Skills Used

| Skill | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Built-in SSH and networking | Connect to the home device and trigger the task |

---

## 💬 Example Prompt


```
(From another device while away) SSH into my home phone and trigger the daily health data collection task.
```
